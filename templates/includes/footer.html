WSaPS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Wachemo Secondary and Preparatory School (WSaPS) respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting all personal information shared on this digital academic portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect student academic records, parent contact details, teacher credentials, and newsletter subscription emails solely for educational and communication purposes.

2. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive information is encrypted and securely stored using enterprise PostgreSQL databases hosted on cloud infrastructure. Authentication data is securely hashed using PBKDF2 algorithms.

3. Communication & Consent

Subscribing to our newsletter or contacting school administration provides consent to receive academic notices and announcements. You may opt-out or request data removal at any time.

Terms of Service

Welcome to WSaPS Academic Portal System. By accessing or using this system, you agree to comply with the following terms:

1. Acceptable Use

Students, teachers, parents, and administrators must maintain academic integrity and account security. Sharing passwords or unauthorized access to system resources is strictly prohibited.

2. Academic Records

Grades, exam results, and attendance records published on the portal are official school records managed under WSaPS institutional policies.

3. System Availability

While WSaPS strives for 99.9% portal uptime, periodic maintenance schedules may be conducted during off-peak hours.
